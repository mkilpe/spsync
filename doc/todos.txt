* packet size caps on the record and s2s connections (found 2026-09-19 with record_data.txt RDS 5, not fixed)
  - serialisation::packet_deserialiser caps a message at 1 MiB by default; the client's record
    connection (comm/net_connection_impl.hpp), the server's client connection (storage_server.cpp)
    and the peer connection (peer_connection.hpp) use the default, while chain_sync batches up to
    max_response_bytes = 8 MiB into one response_records / response_envelopes. A batch above
    1 MiB would throw "max buffer size reached" on the receiver: the range wedge of RD10 at 1 MiB
    instead of the 16 MiB frame. Seen by reading only, no test shows it yet. The template takes
    the limit now (network::max_frame_size, as the data listener does); needs a test with a
    > 1 MiB batch over the wire. Note also the DER codec's 2 MiB limit for one octet string
    (a single metadata value above it cannot be encoded; record data is not affected, chunks
    travel in pieces).
